# 💊 Pharma-Rep Lunch Lane

*Compiled 2026-07-15. The recurring, high-margin catering pipeline: pharmaceutical (and
medical-device) reps buying lunch for doctors' offices to get face time with prescribers.*

## Why this lane
Reps have effectively **unlimited catering budgets** — the lunch is a business-development
expense, not their money. They book the SAME offices on a rotation (a rep "owns" a territory
and feeds those clinics month after month). Win one rep + one office and you're in a repeat
cycle, not a one-off. They are demanding (on-time to the minute, individually labeled, clean
itemized receipt for expense reporting) but they pay full price and tip, and they do not
haggle. Trint hates dealing with people — good: this lane rewards **reliable logistics over
schmoozing**. Nail the delivery and the reorders come by text.

The catch: reliability is the whole game. A late or sloppy drop kills the account permanently.
Our drop-off trays (**The Desk Job / The Roll Call**, no on-site service) are the perfect
product for this — made fresh, delivered hot and labeled, walk away.

## Target offices — the "approved caterer" list
Reps feed **specialists** (high prescribers) hardest — cardiology, endocrinology, rheumatology,
GI, dermatology, allergy/immunology, psychiatry, oncology, orthopedics. Two ways to win these:
be discoverable on ezCater when a rep searches near the address, AND get the **office manager**
to name us when a rep asks "who do you like for lunch?" (office managers gatekeep the rotation).

## The rep-proof operational bar (non-negotiable for this lane)
1. **On time, to the minute.** Reps schedule lunch around a narrow provider window. Late = dead account.
2. **Individually labeled / boxed** where possible — reps love no-mess, grab-and-go for a busy office.
3. **Clean itemized receipt** (Square handles this) — they must expense it with the drug/device company.
4. **Consistent.** Same quality every reorder. This lane is a rotation; consistency compounds.
5. Confirm headcount + delivery window by text the day before; text on drop-off.

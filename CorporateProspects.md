# The Glizzness — Columbia Corporate Catering Prospects

*Internal B2B target list for the **Corporate & Workplace Accounts** lane (prepaid on-site **cart days** + boxed-lunch **drop-offs**). NOT public, NOT on the events map.*
*Built 2026-07-06 from an adversarially-verified research pass. Primary source: [REDI "Boone County Largest Employers" Q1-2024](https://columbiaredi.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Boone-County-Largest-Employers-2025.pdf) + [City of Columbia](https://www.como.gov/about/). Headcounts are Boone County, ~2 yrs old — treat as size signal, not gospel.*

**How to work it:** pitch the **office manager / HR / "culture" or "people" team / facilities**. Offer (a) a standing **cart day** on their lot for a flat prepaid fee, or (b) **boxed-lunch drop-off** for a headcount. Lead with the easy win: a free/discounted first "taste day" to land the account, then recurring.

Why The Glizzness

Not just a hot-dog cart.

More than dogs

Way more than a hot-dog cart

The cart is a full portable kitchen — grill, hot wells, cold wells, a loaded toppings bar. That means a menu that runs well past hot dogs, built fresh to order for any crowd, any dietary need, any budget.

Columbia's own

A hometown brand with a following

We built this on regulars — people who know the cart on sight and the Glizzy by name. Catch us around town, order it on DoorDash, or book the cart for your own crowd.

Our story

From one cart on a corner.

The Glizzness started in 2022 — one cart, a yellow umbrella, and a downtown Columbia street corner. A few winters later we're a Mizzou-campus regular, feeding breweries, weddings, offices, and game days across Boone County. Same bold, laid-back flavor — just a lot more places to find us.
